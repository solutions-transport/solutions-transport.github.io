About the founder

Honesty, reliability and keeping our word to every business partner.

Behind SOLUTION is an experienced entrepreneur with about 8–10 years in Bangladesh's transportation and logistics industry.

Through years of dedication and careful business management, he has built a strong network and long-term relationships with well-known industrial and corporate organizations — including about seven years with companies such as Nahar Agro Group, Faragon Feed Mill, CFB Bangladesh Feed Mill, Jawan Mobil, Gulf Oil and RFL Lubricants.

Under his leadership, SOLUTION has grown to five company-owned vehicles and about 30 more under management. His aim is to keep growing while holding the highest standards of reliability, professionalism and customer satisfaction.

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Send a booking request and ask us in the notes.

Which areas do you cover?

All over Bangladesh. Our base is Mahipal, Feni, and our trucks run to any district.

What kind of trucks do you have?

Covered vans, open-body trucks, dump trucks and heavy-duty vehicles of different load capacities — about 35 in total, 5 of them our own.

How do I book a truck?

Press "Request a truck", fill in the route and load, and send. We'll call you back with the right vehicle and the price.

How is the price set?

It depends on the route, the load and the vehicle. We tell you the price before the trip, so there are no surprises.

Do you work with companies on a regular basis?

Yes. We have worked for about seven years with feed mills and lubricant companies such as Nahar Agro Group, Jawan Mobil and RFL Lubricants.

I have a truck. Can I work with you?

Yes. Create an account as a driver with your vehicle details. Our team will check it and contact you.
